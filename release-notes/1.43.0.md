## New Features

### The V3 Character Sheet (Opt-In)

A brand-new character sheet, built on Foundry's ApplicationV2 framework with Vue 3. Opt-in alongside the existing sheet — pick it per actor from the sheet dropdown; nothing changes unless you switch.

Highlights:

- View vs. edit mode for things that rarely change (name/kin/class/level, max HP and recoveries, backgrounds and icons, ...), with a compact command bar layout for narrow windows.
- Sidebar sections for abilities, backgrounds, icon relationships (with rolling and managing icon benefits) and resources; portrait with a right-click menu and click-to-pick image.
- Stats bar with editable HP/temp HP/recoveries, progress bars, clickable death-save and disengage rolls, recovery spending, and custom resources.
- **Catalog** tab: every power, item and loot entry in expandable rows, with user-configurable tabs (add/rename/reorder), group-by, sort-by and filter-chip controls (with a "hide empty" option), and drag-and-drop reordering.
- **Triggers**, **Effects**, **Loadout** (magic item and feat slot tracks), **Progression** and **Notes** tabs.
- Per-character settings (formerly the v2 sheet's settings tab) moved into their own window, opened from the sheet's controls.
- Feat support per power: a pip per feat, a die icon that rolls the feat and spends a use, click/context-menu use adjustments, and Z-tier markers; equipment attunement as stacked pips for higher-tier items.
- PC defenses gained free-text resistance and vulnerability fields (comma-separated damage types), editable on the v3 sheet's defenses section.
